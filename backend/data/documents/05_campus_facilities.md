# 05. Campus Facilities & Infrastructure

## Central Library & Digital Learning Centre
- **Holdings**: Over 16,000+ volumes, 3,500+ titles, reference encyclopedias, journals, and project theses.
- **Digital Access**: Subscription to **DELNET**, **National Digital Library of India (NDLI)**, and **VTU Consortium** (IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Springer e-journals).
- **Library Timings**:
  - Monday to Friday: 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM
  - Saturday: 8:30 AM to 1:30 PM
- **Circulation Rules**: Undergraduates receive 3 borrower cards; postgraduates receive 4 borrower cards. Loan period is 14 days with renewal options.

## Hostels, Mess & Living
- **Accommodation**: Independent hostel blocks for boys and girls on campus.
- **Facilities**: Well-ventilated rooms, study tables, wardrobes, power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi, solar hot water.
- **Dining**: Hygienic mess serving balanced South and North Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals.
- **Safety & Regulations**: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, security guards, resident wardens, and a strict gate closing curfew of **7:30 PM**.

## Cafeteria & Canteen
- **Timings**: 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM.
- **Menu**: Fresh breakfast (Dosa, Idli, Vada, Poori), afternoon thali meals, Chinese fried rice/noodles, tea, coffee, fruit juices, and snacks.

## College Bus Transportation
- Dedicated fleet connecting Mangaluru City, Surathkal, Moodbidri, Mulki, and Bantwal/B.C. Road.
- Timings: Morning arrival before 8:50 AM; evening departure at 4:40 PM.
- Passes: Semester and annual bus passes available at the accounts counter.

## Sports, Gym & Medical Care
- **Outdoor Sports**: Cricket ground, football ground, volleyball courts, basketball court, athletic tracks.
- **Indoor Games**: Badminton, table tennis, chess, and carrom.
- **Gymnasium**: Equipped fitness center for physical strength and health.
- **Medical Room**: First-aid emergency room with resting beds and emergency ambulance on call.
