# 01. College Information: Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT)

## About the College and Trust History
Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT), Kenjar, Mangaluru, is an esteemed institution established under the stewardship of **Shree Devi Education Trust**. Founded by visionary educationist and philanthropist **Sri A. Sadananda Shetty** (Chairman), along with **Sri Nidhish S. Shetty** (Vice Chairman), **Smt. Maina S. Shetty** (Secretary), and **Smt. Priyanka N. Shetty** (Trustee), the Trust has played a defining role in advancing higher technical, paramedical, and management education in coastal Karnataka.

SDIT is situated on a verdant 15+ acre campus along Airport Road, Kenjar, near Mangaluru International Airport.

## Vision
To evolve into a center of excellence in engineering and management education, pioneering innovative research, nurturing entrepreneurial flair, and shaping globally competent professionals with strong ethical and social values.

## Mission
- Impart high-caliber, multidisciplinary technical education through industry-aligned curriculum and modern pedagogies.
- Cultivate analytical minds, research acumen, and leadership qualities among students.
- Foster sustainable industry-academia collaborations to enhance real-world problem-solving and employability.
- Instill a spirit of social commitment, environmental stewardship, and professional integrity.

## Accreditations, Affiliations & Recognitions
- **Affiliation**: Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.
- **Approval**: Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- **Government Recognition**: Recognized by the Department of Higher Education, Government of Karnataka.
- **Research Recognition**: Accredited as official Research Centres by VTU in core engineering disciplines.
