# 04. Events, Activities & Student Clubs

## Flagship College Fest: 'Sambram'
'Sambram' is SDIT's premier inter-collegiate cultural fest, renowned across coastal Karnataka:
- **Events**: Eastern & Western Group Dance, Battle of the Bands, Solo Classical/Western Vocal, Street Play, Thematic Fashion Show, Beatboxing, Photography, and Fine Arts.
- **Participation**: Draws vibrant talent from across Karnataka and neighboring states.

## Technical Symposia, Hackathons & Workshops
- **Departmental Tech Fests**: Annual symposiums hosted by CSE, ECE, Aeronautical, and Mechanical associations.
- **Hackathons**: 24-hour software code-sprints and IoT prototyping hackathons.
- **Workshops**: Hands-on bootcamps in Generative AI, Drone Building, Cloud Architectures, and Autonomous Robotics.

## Active Student Clubs & Associations
The official SDIT clubs page lists the following active clubs:

1. **Lingua Nova**: Enhances language proficiency through interactive sessions and workshops in verbal and written communication. It also develops public speaking, negotiation, and business correspondence skills.
2. **Media Mavericks**: Covers photography, videography, camera techniques, editing software, creative projects, industry trends, and hands-on experience in different media formats.
3. **Coders Nexus**: Teaches Python, Java, and C++ through coding challenges, hackathons, and software-development workshops for coders of all levels.
4. **Sports Mania**: Organizes sports activities, friendly matches, and tournaments, including football and volleyball, while promoting fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship.
5. **Cult-mosaic**: Promotes diversity and cultural exchange through activities and events that build cultural awareness, understanding, appreciation, unity, and harmony.
6. **Finance Club**: Explores investment strategies, financial markets, and wealth management while building students' financial skills and insights.
7. **Marketing Club (MAntra)**: Develops marketing skills through workshops, competitions, and real-world projects, helping students become creative and capable marketers.
8. **HR (HRiday)**: Nurtures talent and leadership while helping students explore human resources, gain practical skills, and prepare for HR careers.
9. **Samarpan**: Focuses on compassion, empowerment, social change, and community outreach to help create a more inclusive future.
10. **Eco Club (Sankalp)**: Works toward a sustainable future through conservation, environmental awareness, and positive action in the community.

Source: [SDIT Clubs](https://sdit.ac.in/clubs/)
