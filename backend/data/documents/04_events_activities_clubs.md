# 04. Events, Activities & Student Clubs

## Flagship College Fest: 'Sambram'
'Sambram' is SDIT's premier inter-collegiate cultural fest, renowned across coastal Karnataka:
- **Events**: Eastern & Western Group Dance, Battle of the Bands, Solo Classical/Western Vocal, Street Play, Thematic Fashion Show, Beatboxing, Photography, and Fine Arts.
- **Participation**: Draws vibrant talent from across Karnataka and neighboring states.

## Technical Symposia, Hackathons & Workshops
- **Departmental Tech Fests**: Annual symposiums hosted by CSE, ECE, Aeronautical, and Mechanical associations.
- **Hackathons**: 24-hour software code-sprints and IoT prototyping hackathons.
- **Workshops**: Hands-on bootcamps in Generative AI, Drone Building, Cloud Architectures, and Autonomous Robotics.

## Active Student Clubs & Associations
1. **Coding & Open-Source Club**: Weekly algorithmic programming challenges, peer code reviews, Git/GitHub workshops, and open-source contributions.
2. **Robotics & IoT Innovation Club**: Focuses on line-follower bots, quadcopters, microcontroller circuits (ESP32/Arduino/Raspberry Pi), and industrial automation.
3. **Aeronautical Aero-Design Society**: Aeromodelling competitions, flight design, and RC plane dynamics.
4. **IEEE Student Branch**: Organizes national webinars, student conferences, and paper presentations.
5. **Computer Society of India (CSI)**: Workshops on cutting-edge software paradigms and networking.
6. **National Service Scheme (NSS)**: Cleanliness drives, rural education outreach, tree plantation drives, and social welfare programs.
7. **Youth Red Cross**: Regular on-campus voluntary blood donation drives in association with local Mangaluru blood banks.
