# 03. Admissions & Academics

## Admission Eligibility & Channels

### Undergraduate (B.E. 4-Years)
- **Minimum Qualification**: Passed 10+2 / 2nd PUC / CBSE / ICSE or recognized equivalent examination.
- **Mandatory Subjects**: Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus one optional subject from Chemistry / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics / Biotechnology.
- **Marks Requirement**:
  - General Merit Candidates: Minimum **45% aggregate** in optional subjects.
  - Reserved Categories (SC, ST, Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B of Karnataka): Minimum **40% aggregate**.
- **Lateral Entry**: 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma holders with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category) are eligible for direct admission to the 2nd Year (3rd Semester).

### Postgraduate (MBA, MCA, M.Tech)
- **MBA**: 3-year Bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate (45% for Karnataka SC/ST/Cat-1) and valid rank in Karnataka PGCET / KMAT / MAT.
- **MCA**: Bachelor's degree with Mathematics/Statistics at 10+2 or Degree level with 50% marks (45% reserved) and valid PGCET / KMAT score.
- **M.Tech**: B.E./B.Tech in relevant domain with 50% marks (45% reserved) and valid GATE / PGCET score.

## Fee Structure, Concessions & Scholarships
- **Government Fees**: As determined by the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) for KCET/PGCET allotments.
- **Scholarship Schemes**:
  - **State Scholarship Portal (SSP)**: Karnataka government post-matric scholarships for SC/ST/OBC/Minority students.
  - **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**: Central government merit and minority scholarships.
  - **Vidyasiri / E-Pass**: Boarding and lodging support from the backward classes welfare department.
  - **Shree Devi Education Trust Scholarships**: Merit concessions awarded to high-scoring students and defense/sports achievers.

## Academic Calendar, Examinations & Results
- **Institutional Calendar of Events**: Issued prior to each semester detailing CIE test dates, holidays, seminars, and semester closing.
- **Examination Scheme**:
  - Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 3 internal assessments conducted by the college examination committee.
  - Semester End Examination (SEE): University theory and practical examinations conducted under VTU.
- **Results**: Officially published on the VTU portal: **https://results.vtu.ac.in**.
