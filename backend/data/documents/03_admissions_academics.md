# 03. Admissions, Fee Structure & Academic Calendar

## Admission Eligibility & Channels

### Undergraduate (B.E. 4-Years / 8 Semesters)
- **Minimum Qualification**: Passed 10+2 / Karnataka 2nd PUC / CBSE / ICSE or recognized equivalent examination.
- **Mandatory Subjects**: Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Computer Science / Electronics / Biology / Biotechnology.
- **Marks Requirement**:
  - General Merit (GM) Candidates: Minimum **45% aggregate** in optional subjects.
  - Reserved Categories (Karnataka SC, ST, Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B): Minimum **40% aggregate**.
- **Lateral Entry**: 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma holders with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category) are eligible for direct admission to the 2nd Year (3rd Semester).

### Postgraduate (MBA, MCA, M.Tech)
- **MBA (2-Years)**: 3-year Bachelor's degree with minimum 50% aggregate (45% for Karnataka SC/ST/Cat-1) and valid rank in Karnataka PGCET / KMAT / MAT / CMAT.
- **MCA (2-Years)**: Bachelor's degree (BCA, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A.) with Mathematics/Statistics at 10+2 or Degree level with 50% marks (45% reserved) and valid PGCET / KMAT rank.
- **M.Tech (2-Years)**: B.E./B.Tech in relevant branch with 50% marks (45% reserved) and valid GATE / Karnataka PGCET score.

## Comprehensive Fee Structure

Fees at SDIT are determined on the basis of admission quota (Government / COMEDK / Management) as governed by the Government of Karnataka and Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) guidelines.

### 1. B.E. / B.Tech Annual Tuition Fee Structure (Approximate Guidelines)
- **Government Quota (via KCET - Code E146)**:
  - Prescribed KEA Government fee: **₹65,000 to ₹95,000 per year** (including university registration & examination fees).
  - Supernumerary Quota (SNQ): Government tuition fee waiver for meritorious low-income students (nominal fee only).
- **COMEDK Quota**:
  - Engineering programs: **₹1,80,000 to ₹2,40,000 per year** (depending on the specialization branch).
- **Management Quota (Institutional Admission)**:
  - Computer Science (CSE), AIML, AI & DS: Approximately **₹1,75,000 to ₹2,50,000 per year**.
  - Information Science (ISE), Electronics & Communication (ECE): Approximately **₹1,40,000 to ₹1,80,000 per year**.
  - Aeronautical, Mechanical, Civil Engineering: Approximately **₹1,00,000 to ₹1,40,000 per year**.
  *(Note: Management fee structure is subject to seat availability, 12th PCM merit, and annual trust notifications).*

### 2. Postgraduate Tuition Fee Structure
- **MBA (Master of Business Administration - 2 Years)**:
  - PGCET Quota (Code B281): **₹55,000 to ₹75,000 per year**.
  - Management Quota: **₹85,000 to ₹1,25,000 per year**.
- **MCA (Master of Computer Applications - 2 Years)**:
  - PGCET Quota (Code C476): **₹55,000 to ₹75,000 per year**.
  - Management Quota: **₹80,000 to ₹1,20,000 per year**.
- **M.Tech (Construction Technology - 2 Years)**:
  - PGCET / GATE Quota (Code T868): **₹65,000 to ₹80,000 per year**.

### 3. Hostel & Mess Fee Structure
- **Accommodation & Dining**:
  - Separate on-campus hostels for Boys and Girls.
  - Annual Fee: Approximately **₹65,000 to ₹80,000 per year** (covers fully furnished room, 3 wholesome meals/day + evening tea, 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, water, and power backup).

### 4. Campus Bus Transportation Fees
- College operates a fleet of luxury buses covering Mangaluru city and coastal feeder routes.
- Annual Fee: Approximately **₹15,000 to ₹28,000 per year** depending on pickup stop:
  - Mangaluru City / State Bank / Hampankatta / Kankanady: ~₹16,000/yr
  - Surathkal / NITK / Mukka / Mulki: ~₹18,000 to ₹22,000/yr
  - Moodbidri / Kaikamba / Bajpe: ~₹15,000 to ₹18,000/yr
  - Bantwal / B.C. Road / Farangipete: ~₹22,000 to ₹26,000/yr
  - Ullal / Thokkottu / Deralakatte: ~₹24,000 to ₹28,000/yr

### 5. Scholarship Schemes & Financial Assistance
- **State Scholarship Portal (SSP)**: Post-matric scholarships from Karnataka Government for SC/ST/OBC/Minority students providing up to 100% tuition reimbursement.
- **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**: Central government merit-cum-means scholarships for minorities and AICTE Pragati & Saksham schemes.
- **Vidyasiri / E-Pass**: Karnataka Backward Classes Welfare department hostel & food fee support.
- **Shree Devi Education Trust Merit Scholarships**: Fee concessions awarded by the management for high-ranking KCET/PUC students and national sports/defense achievers.

## Academic Calendar & Calendar of Events

SDIT's academic calendar is formulated by the **Academic Advisory Board** under the leadership of the Principal and Heads of Departments, in strict alignment with the **Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)** university calendar.

### Odd Semester Timeline (Semesters I, III, V, VII)
- **Commencement of Semester Classes**: Mid-August / September
- **Student Induction Program (SIP - 1st Year)**: First 2 weeks of term (campus familiarization, mentor allocation, bridge courses)
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation - 1 (CIE-1 / IA-1)**: 4th to 5th week of term
- **First Attendance & Performance Review (85% VTU Attendance Rule)**: 6th week
- **Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM)**: Following IA-1 result analysis
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation - 2 (CIE-2 / IA-2)**: 9th to 10th week of term
- **Department Technical Symposiums & Hackathons**: October / November
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation - 3 (CIE-3 / IA-3)**: 13th to 14th week of term
- **Laboratory Internal Practical Tests & Project Submissions**: 14th to 15th week
- **Last Working Day of the Semester**: Early December (for higher sems) / January (for 1st year)
- **VTU Practical / Viva-Voce Examinations**: First 2 weeks of January
- **VTU Semester End Theory Examinations (SEE)**: Mid-January to February
- **Official VTU Results**: Published on **https://results.vtu.ac.in**

### Even Semester Timeline (Semesters II, IV, VI, VIII)
- **Commencement of Even Semester Classes**: Mid-February / March
- **First Internal Assessment (CIE-1)**: Late March / Early April
- **Annual College Cultural Fest 'Sambram' & Sports Day**: April
- **Second Internal Assessment (CIE-2)**: Late April / Early May
- **Technical Project Exhibition & Paper Presentations**: May
- **Third Internal Assessment (CIE-3)**: Late May / Early June
- **Laboratory Practical Examinations & Capstone Evaluations**: June
- **Last Working Day of Even Semester**: Early June
- **VTU Semester End Theory Examinations (SEE)**: Mid-June to July
- **Summer Vacation & Mandatory Industry Internships**: July to August

### Access to Official Academic Calendars & PDF Circulars
- The printed Calendar of Events is handed over to every student at the start of each semester and displayed on department notice boards.
- Download updates and circulars from the official website: **[https://sdit.ac.in](https://sdit.ac.in)** and **[https://vtu.ac.in](https://vtu.ac.in)**.
