# 09. Official Programs Syllabus PDF Directory

Source: [SDIT Programs](https://sdit.ac.in/) and the official SDIT program pages.

SDIT publishes syllabus material as official VTU scheme PDFs. The first year of B.E. is shared across engineering programs through the common first-year PDF. Higher-semester content is provided through branch-specific scheme PDFs where available. The labels below are the scheme years published by SDIT, not separate first-year, second-year, third-year, and fourth-year files.
