# 06. Training & Placement Department

## Placement Statistics & Highlights
- High placement rate of 85–90% for eligible graduating students.
- Prominent multinational IT giants, consulting firms, and engineering conglomerates recruit regularly from SDIT.
- Competitive salary packages: Top packages range from 8 to 14+ LPA; strong average salary packages across computing, core, and management disciplines.

## Visiting Recruiters
- **Tech Giants & IT Services**: Amazon, Infosys, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Wipro, Cognizant, DXC Technology, Capgemini, SLK Software, Mindtree, Robosoft Technologies, Novigo Solutions, Hexaware Technologies, 6D Technologies.
- **Banking & Finance**: ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, HDFC, Kotak Mahindra.
- **Core Engineering**: Bosch vendors, Quest Global, L&T Technology Services, Subex, local aeronautical design vendors.

## 4-Tier Placement Training Program
1. **Semester 3 & 4 (Foundational)**:
   - Verbal communication, corporate etiquette, body language, and confidence building.
2. **Semester 5 (Aptitude Mastery)**:
   - Quantitative mathematics, logical reasoning, critical thinking, and graphical data analysis.
3. **Semester 6 (Technical Bootcamps)**:
   - Coding in Python, Java, Data Structures, Algorithms, Full-Stack, and System Design.
4. **Semester 7 & 8 (Interview Simulations)**:
   - Company-specific test mock sessions, technical panel mock interviews with industry experts, HR interview simulations, and resume refinement.

## Internships
The placement cell actively assists pre-final year students in securing summer and semester internships at leading IT corporations and manufacturing plants.
