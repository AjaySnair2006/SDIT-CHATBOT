# 02. Departments, Faculty & Courses Offered

## Engineering & Management Departments

### 1. Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- **Head of Department (HOD)**: Prof. Anand S. Uppar, M.Tech, (Ph.D.)
- **Key Specializations**: High-performance computing, distributed networks, cybersecurity, algorithms, full-stack systems.
- **Laboratories**: Data Structures Lab, Algorithms & Cloud Computing Center, Network Security Lab, AI Programming Lab.

### 2. Department of CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning - AIML)
- **Focus**: Supervised/unsupervised deep neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, robotic cognitive systems.
- **Laboratories**: GPU-accelerated Machine Learning Lab, Computer Vision & Robotics Studio.

### 3. Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS)
- **Focus**: Big data pipelines, statistical modeling, predictive analytics, enterprise data warehousing.

### 4. Department of Information Science & Engineering (ISE)
- **Focus**: Cloud architectures, web service design, software quality assurance, mobile computing.

### 5. Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- **Head of Department (HOD)**: Prof. Chitra Prabhu, M.Tech
- **Focus**: Embedded IoT, VLSI chip design, digital signal processing, satellite and wireless communications.
- **Laboratories**: Digital Electronics Lab, Advanced Microprocessor Lab, VLSI & IoT Studio, Communication Circuits Lab.

### 6. Department of Aeronautical Engineering
- **Focus**: Aerodynamics, propulsion systems, structural aerospace mechanics, flight simulation, UAV design.
- **Special Facilities**: Subsonic Wind Tunnel, Aircraft Propulsion Testbed, Avionics Simulation Laboratory.

### 7. Department of Mechanical Engineering
- **Head of Department (HOD)**: Prof. Sudheer Kumar B. N., M.Tech
- **Focus**: CAD/CAM/CAE, thermodynamics, robotic automation, fluid machinery, manufacturing sciences.
- **Laboratories**: Machine Shop, CNC Machining Center, Fluid Mechanics & Heat Transfer Lab, Materials Testing Lab.

### 8. Department of Civil Engineering
- **Focus**: Structural engineering, GIS & surveying, concrete technology, environmental and geotechnical engineering.
- **Laboratories**: Geotechnical Lab, Concrete & Highway Materials Testing Lab, Total Station Surveying Suite.

### 9. Department of Business Administration (MBA)
- **Specializations**: Marketing Management, Financial Management, and Human Resource Management (HRM).
- **Pedagogy**: Case study analysis, live business consulting projects, corporate guest lectures.

### 10. Department of Computer Applications (MCA)
- **Focus**: Modern full-stack development, mobile app architectures, enterprise database management, cloud computing.

### 11. M.Tech & Ph.D. Research Centers
- **M.Tech Specializations**: Construction Technology, Computer Science & Engineering.
- **Ph.D. Programs**: VTU recognized research centers offering doctoral research with faculty guides.

## Syllabus Overview (VTU CBCS Scheme)
The college operates under the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Choice Based Credit System (CBCS). Core theory courses are paired with mandatory practical labs, continuous internal evaluation (CIE), open electives, and an 8th-semester industry capstone project.
