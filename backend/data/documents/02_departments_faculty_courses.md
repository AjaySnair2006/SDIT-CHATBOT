# 02. Departments, Faculty & Courses Offered

## Comprehensive Department Details & Programs Offered

Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT), Kenjar, Mangaluru, offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across 10 specialized departments.

---

### 1. Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- **Degree**: Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) — 4 Years (8 Semesters)
- **Intake**: 180 Seats
- **Head of Department (HOD)**: Prof. Anand S. Uppar, M.Tech, (Ph.D.)
- **Key Specializations**: High-performance computing, distributed cloud systems, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, full-stack software development.
- **Laboratories & Infrastructure**:
  - Data Structures & Algorithms Lab
  - Cloud Computing & High-Performance Computing Center
  - Network Security & Cryptography Lab
  - Object-Oriented Programming (Java/Python) Studio
  - AI & Machine Learning Programming Lab
- **Syllabus & Curriculum**: Based on VTU 2025/2026 CBCS Scheme. Includes subjects like DSA, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, DBMS, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and Full Stack Development.
- **Official Syllabus PDF**: [Download CSE Syllabus PDF](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/themes/shridevi-child/pdf/COMPUTER-SCIENCE-HIGHER-SEM.pdf)
- **Webpage**: `https://sdit.ac.in/computer-science-engineering/`

### 9. Department of Business Administration (MBA)
- **Degree**: Master of Business Administration (MBA) — 2 Years (4 Semesters)
- **Intake**: 120 Seats
- **Eligibility**: Any 3-year Bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate (45% for Karnataka SC/ST/Cat-1) + valid rank in Karnataka PGCET (Code: **B281**) / KMAT / MAT / CMAT.
- **Specializations**:
  - **Financial Management** (Investment Banking, Derivatives, Corporate Finance)
  - **Marketing Management** (Digital Marketing, Brand Management, Retail Sales)
  - **Human Resource Management (HRM)** (Talent Analytics, Labor Law, Leadership)
- **Pedagogy**: Harvard case study methodology, live business consulting projects, weekly corporate guest lectures, industrial visits.
- **Official Syllabus PDF**: [Download MBA Syllabus PDF](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/themes/shridevi-child/pdf/MBA-new.pdf) | [Download MBA 2024 Scheme PDF](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/MBA-2024-SCHEME-COs.pdf)
- **Webpage**: `https://sdit.ac.in/master-of-business-administration/`
