# 08. Syllabus Overview, Subject Details & VTU Curriculum

## Overview of VTU CBCS Curriculum & Scheme

Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT), Kenjar, Mangaluru, is affiliated with **Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi**, approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and recognized by the Government of Karnataka. The college adheres to the VTU **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** and **Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)**.

- **Total Credits for B.E. Degree**: 160 Credits (across 8 semesters; ~20–22 credits per semester).
- **Evaluation Scheme**:
  - **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)**: 50 Marks (3 written Internal Assessment tests + assignments + quizzes/lab CIE).
  - **Semester End Examination (SEE)**: 50 Marks (University theory/practical exam).
  - **Passing Minimum**: Minimum 40% in SEE and 40% aggregate (CIE + SEE) per course.
- **Mandatory Non-Credit Courses (NCMC)**: National Service Scheme (NSS) / Physical Education / Yoga / Indian Constitution / Cyber Law / Environmental Studies.
- **AICTE Activity Points**: Students must earn **100 activity points** between semesters 1 to 8 as a requirement for degree award (75 points for lateral entry).

## 1st Year B.E. Common Syllabus & Subject Details

The first year of engineering is structured into two balanced cycles: **Physics Group** and **Chemistry Group**.

### I / II Semester: Physics Group Subjects
- **Applied Mathematics - I / II** (`1BMATx101 / 1BMATx201` - 4 Credits): Differential calculus, linear algebra, vector calculus, partial differential equations, and numerical methods.
- **Applied Physics for Engineering** (`1BPHYx102` - 4 Credits): Lasers, optical fibers, quantum mechanics, electrical and dielectric properties, material semiconductors with Integrated Physics Lab.
- **Computer-Aided Engineering Drawing (CAED)** (`1BCEDx103` - 3 Credits): Orthographic projection, isometric views, 2D/3D modeling using modern CAD software.
- **Engineering Science Course (ESC-I)** (`1BXXX104x` - 3 Credits): Principles of Basic Electrical Engineering or Basic Electronics.
- **Programme Specific Course (PSC)** (`1Bxxx105x` - 3 Credits): Principles of Programming Using C or Introduction to Python Programming.
- **Soft Skills** (`1BSKS106` - Non-Credit Mandatory Course): Personality development, business communication, and interview skills.
- **Programming Laboratory** (`1BxxxL107x` - 1 Credit): Hands-on programming implementation.
- **Innovation & Design Thinking Lab (IDT)** (`1BIDTL158` - 1 Credit): Project-based experiential learning and creative problem-solving.
- **Kannada Language** (`1BKSK109 / 1BKBK109` - 1 Credit): *Samskrutika Kannada* (for Karnataka students) / *Balake Kannada* (Conversational Kannada for non-Karnataka students).

### I / II Semester: Chemistry Group Subjects
- **Applied Mathematics - II** (`1BMATx201` - 4 Credits): Integral calculus, multiple integrals, Laplace transforms, and complex variables.
- **Applied Chemistry for Engineering** (`1BCHEx102` - 4 Credits): Electrochemistry, energy storage systems (batteries), corrosion science, polymers, nanomaterials with Integrated Chemistry Lab.
- **Elements of Mechanical / Civil Engineering** (3 Credits): Basic thermodynamic systems, manufacturing, structural mechanics, and survey concepts.
- **Professional Writing Skills in English** (`1BPWS106` - 1 Credit): Technical documentation and phonetics.
- **Scientific Foundation of Health (SFH)** (`1BSFH108` - 1 Credit): Health, wellness, nutrition, and mental health basics.
- **Constitution of India & Cyber Law (CIP)** (`1BCIP109` - 1 Credit): Fundamental rights, state policy, intellectual property rights, and cyber crime prevention.

## Master of Business Administration (MBA) — Semester-Wise Subjects

2-Year Full-Time Postgraduate Program (4 Semesters) affiliated to VTU.

- **Semester I (Core Foundation)**:
  - `3MBA101`: Management and Organizational Behaviour (4 Credits)
  - `3MBA102`: Accounting for Managers (4 Credits)
  - `3MBA103`: Managerial Economics (4 Credits)
  - `3MBA104`: Business Statistics and Analytics (4 Credits)
  - `3MBA105`: Marketing Management (4 Credits)
  - `3MBA106`: Managerial Communication (3 Credits)
- **Semester II (Core Functional)**:
  - Human Resource Management (HRM)
  - Financial Management
  - Operations Research & Supply Chain Management
  - Business Research Methods & Quantitative Decision Making
  - Entrepreneurship Development & Startups
- **Semester III & IV (Dual Specializations)**:
  - **Finance**: Investment Management, Corporate Valuation, Banking & Financial Services, Derivatives.
  - **Marketing**: Consumer Behaviour, Digital & Social Media Marketing, Brand Management, Retail & Services Marketing.
  - **Human Resource Management**: Talent Acquisition, Performance Management, Industrial Relations & Labour Laws, Organizational Leadership.
  - **Internship**: 6-week Summer Internship & 4th Semester Major Business Research Project.
