# 07. Student Assistance, Grievances & Helpdesk

## New Student Orientation & FAQs
- **Student Induction Program (SIP)**: 2-week familiarization program with campus tours, bridge classes, and mentor introductions.
- **ID Card & Smart Cards**: Issued during the first week; compulsory to wear on campus with college lanyard.
- **Faculty Mentorship**: Each student is assigned a personal faculty mentor/counselor who guides academic progress and attends to student welfare.

## Academic Processes
- **Student Leave Application**:
  1. Procure Leave Form from your Department Office.
  2. Complete details with parent consent letter or medical certificate for leaves exceeding 3 days.
  3. Obtain signature from Mentor and HOD for formal recording.
- **Attendance Requirement**: Minimum 85% attendance across every course is mandatory under VTU rules.
- **Exam Hall Tickets**: Distributed by the college exam cell prior to university examinations.
- **Official Documentation**: Bonafide certificates, transfer certificates, and fee receipts can be requested at the administrative counters in the Administrative Block.

## Grievance Redressal & Anti-Ragging
- **Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging**: Strict adherence to Supreme Court guidelines and the Karnataka Education Act. Any act of harassment or humiliation is dealt with immediately.
- **4-Step Grievance Procedure**:
  - **Online**: Lodge complaints via the official grievance portal at **https://sdit.ac.in/grievance/**.
  - **Offline**: Drop written grievances into the Suggestion/Grievance Box in the Admin Block.
  - Complaints are registered, investigated, and addressed with written resolutions within 48 to 72 hours.
